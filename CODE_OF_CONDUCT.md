# Code of Conduct

Contributors must participate in a professional, respectful, evidence-focused manner.

Harassment, threats, doxxing, discriminatory abuse, deliberate misrepresentation of expertise, and publication of another person's sensitive personal information are not acceptable.

Legal and policy disagreements should be resolved by narrowing scope, improving sources, documenting uncertainty, and seeking qualified local review rather than by asserting unsupported certainty.

Repository maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate these rules and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or the integrity of the corpus.
