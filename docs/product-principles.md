# Product principles

## Mission

Make it materially easier to establish and operate independent, public-benefit research institutions by publishing free, source-linked jurisdiction guides, reusable governance templates, and a transparent maintenance workflow.

## Critical boundary

The product must not promise that a user can “form a nonprofit anywhere in the world.” It provides:

1. a jurisdiction applicability assessment;
2. a human-readable local process guide;
3. a machine-readable requirements graph;
4. reusable but explicitly non-localized templates; and
5. a handoff path to qualified local legal, tax, accounting, banking, and compliance review.

“Nonprofit” is not treated as a globally uniform legal category. A jurisdiction may require a foundation, association, trust, non-stock company, public-benefit entity, tax recognition, charity registration, or a combination.

## Institutional goal

The core archetype is an independent, curiosity-driven research centre that protects researcher time from teaching and avoidable administrative overhead. The institutional design is portable. Entity form, tax treatment, governance requirements, employment, fundraising, and reporting are local implementation questions.

## Interface rule

The intake should be a research-centre design interview, not a legal-form quiz. The output should show candidate routes, uncertainties, blockers, sources, and counsel questions rather than a personalized legal conclusion.
