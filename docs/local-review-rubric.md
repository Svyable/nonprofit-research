# Local review rubric

A local review is a scoped factual review of a jurisdiction packet. It is not a blanket certification of the project, a promise that a route fits every founder, or a substitute for client-specific advice.

## Reviewer scope

A reviewer must state the scope they are reviewing, including:

- jurisdiction and any relevant parent/subnational boundary;
- legal route or route component reviewed;
- subject area, such as formation, charity/tax recognition, fundraising, governance, employment, or cross-border activity;
- source cutoff or verification date; and
- professional role relevant to that scope.

Do not infer expertise outside the declared scope.

## Minimum checks for local-expert-reviewed content

Before promoting an item to `local_expert_reviewed`, the reviewer should confirm that:

1. the jurisdiction boundary is correct;
2. the cited official source is the right authority for the proposition;
3. the locator or excerpt supports the structured claim as written;
4. entity formation is not conflated with tax, charitable, or fundraising recognition;
5. prerequisites and ordering are represented accurately in the requirements graph;
6. stated official fees and recurring obligations have an appropriate freshness interval;
7. uncertainty, exceptions, and unresolved specialist questions remain visible; and
8. the text does not become individualized legal advice merely because it is accurate in the abstract.

## Conflicts and independence

Reviewers should disclose material conflicts that could reasonably affect the review, including paid relationships with a service provider whose product or route is featured. A reviewer may contribute research, but substantive local-law promotion should receive an independent second set of eyes where practical.

## Review outcomes

- **Approve for source check:** evidence is adequately classified and represented, but local professional review has not occurred.
- **Approve for local expert review:** the scoped item satisfies the local-review gate.
- **Request changes:** the proposition, scope, dependency graph, or source support needs correction.
- **Escalate:** specialist review is needed for tax, sanctions, export controls, regulated data/research, cross-border grants, employment/immigration, political activity, or another high-risk area.
- **Withdraw/stale:** the item should not be presented as current.

## Recording the review

The packet records reviewer **roles** and dates; GitHub pull-request history records who performed the review and the discussion. Avoid placing unnecessary personal contact information or professional-identification documents in the corpus.

A review should identify the affected claim, route, requirement, or obligation IDs so later corrections can be traced precisely.
