# Legal safety policy

Research Centre Atlas is an informational knowledge commons, not a law firm, filing agent, tax preparer, bank, sanctions screener, or incorporation service.

## The platform may

- describe legal forms and regulatory routes that exist in a scoped jurisdiction;
- summarize official filing sequences, dependencies, fees, and recurring obligations;
- compare published route attributes without endorsing a route for a specific user;
- surface uncertainty and review triggers;
- generate a counsel-handoff packet containing the user's institutional design choices and unresolved questions; and
- provide reusable governance templates explicitly labeled non-localized.

## The platform must not

- calculate a person's or organization's legal eligibility;
- submit government filings or make attestations on a user's behalf;
- claim a route is automatically charitable, tax-exempt, compliant, or suitable;
- recommend evasion of registration, tax, sanctions, AML/CFT, disclosure, fundraising, beneficial-ownership, or reporting controls;
- ingest or expose passports, signatures, beneficial-owner records, or sensitive incorporation documents; or
- replace qualified local professional review.

## Mandatory escalation flags

Mark `specialist_review_required` or an equivalent unresolved state when facts involve cross-border grants or donors, sanctions exposure, politically exposed persons, lobbying or political activity, restricted or dual-use research, export controls, regulated personal/health/genomic/financial data, employees or immigration, complex tax residence, or regulated fundraising.

Compliance controls should be proportionate and risk-based. The corpus must not imply that all nonprofit organizations present the same financial-crime risk.
